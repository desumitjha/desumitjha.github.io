    Ask my portfolio. Or paste your job ad.

    A small retrieval-augmented generation pipeline over facts about me. You see every step: what it retrieved, how similar it was, the grounded prompt a model would receive, and the answer. If nothing matches, it says so instead of making things up.

    Retrieval here uses TF‑IDF word overlap so it runs without a server. My real projects use neural embeddings (3072‑dimension vectors), but the retrieve → augment → generate steps are the same.
