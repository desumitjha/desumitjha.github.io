About

From messy data to reliable AI

I am a senior data engineer with a background in analytics and consulting across India and Germany. I build lakehouse platforms (Azure Databricks, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog), data pipelines and governance.

I am moving into applied AI: LLM applications, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), agentic workflows and tool use. I learn by building small projects and writing down what I learn, and I am preparing for the Claude Certified Architect – Professional (CCAR-P) certification.

I work best between business and IT: finding the right AI use case, judging its value and feasibility, and then building it on a governed data foundation. I am open to AI Solutions, AI Consultant and applied AI engineering roles in Germany that mix stakeholder work with hands-on building.
